Questions

Do you need my password?

Never. We only need the public link to your profile or post. Keep your account public while an order is running.

How fast is delivery?

Most orders start within minutes to a few hours, depending on the service and quantity. You can follow progress in your orders list.

Will the results last?

Delivery comes from third-party providers and social platforms can remove accounts or followers at any time. Services with the refill tag are topped up if the count drops within the stated period. We can’t guarantee permanence — see our terms.

How do I pay?

Nigerian customers pay in Naira by card, bank transfer or USSD, from ₦500. Customers abroad can pay with crypto (USDT, USDC, Bitcoin and others).

Terms & refund policy

Last updated 2026.

What we do

Boostrize resells social media growth services (such as followers, likes, comments and reposts) supplied by third-party providers. Delivery speed and results depend on those providers and on the social platforms.

No guarantee of permanence

Social platforms can remove followers, likes or accounts at any time, and their rules may restrict the use of services like ours. You use Boostrize at your own risk and are responsible for following the rules of the platforms you use. Where a service shows a refill tag, the provider replenishes drops within the stated period; other services have no drop protection.

Your account and links

We never ask for your social media password. Give us a public link, keep the account or post public until the order finishes, and double-check the link: we can’t refund orders placed on the wrong link, or on a private or deleted account.

Refunds

  • If an order fails or is canceled before delivery, the full amount returns to your wallet automatically.
  • If an order is only partly delivered, the undelivered part is refunded automatically.
  • Completed orders are not refundable. Do not run two orders for the same link at the same time.
  • Refunds are credited to your Boostrize wallet balance. Wallet funds are for spending on our services.

Payments

Balances are held in US dollars; Naira top-ups are converted at the rate shown on the Add funds page at the time you pay; crypto top-ups are priced in US dollars and credited after the payment is confirmed on the network (send the exact amount shown, underpayments and late payments may need support to resolve). Top-ups are non-refundable once credited, except where required by law.

Acceptable use

Don’t use Boostrize for illegal content, harassment, scams, or anything that breaks the law or a platform’s rules. We may refuse or cancel orders and disable accounts that do.

Liability

Services are provided “as is”. To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the amount you paid for the affected order.

Contact

Questions? Contact our support team.
